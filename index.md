# RAPID

RAPID (Roman Alerts Promptly from Image Differencing) is a NASA-funded
Project Infrastructure Team for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope,
based at Caltech/IPAC. RAPID is built to deliver image differencing of
every new calibrated Roman image, a public alert stream of transient and variable
candidates carrying alert-driven forced-photometry histories, and
public source catalogs.

Roman's mission requirements include supernova cosmology and
microlensing, but not other time-domain astronomy. RAPID fills that
gap, extracting broader transient and variability science from surveys
built for other purposes. Its mandate is to support time-domain
science, not produce official science results. Its outputs are public,
for the entire time-domain astronomy community.

This site is the public reference for RAPID. Reference pages are
updated in place: the System section states the design target the
pipeline is built to, and the Products section, when it exists, states
what is available. Posts in the log are dated and never revised. The
site is being built up section by section.
