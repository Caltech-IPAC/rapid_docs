# The X-path baseline, 2026-11

The X path makes alerts from `xsources`, `xobjects` and `xmemberships`,
the rebuild's ports of `dev`'s XSources, XAstroObjects and XMerges
({ref}`ruling <decision-packets-from-xsources>`). On 2026-11-08 the
rebuild ran it once, as run 169, over the 72 images of Russ Laher's
10/20/2026 pilot slice. On 2026-11-09 Russ ran `dev` on IMSS over the
same 72 images as `procreqs` reqid 8. This post sets the two runs side by
side and scores both against the fake-source truth. It is the team's
starting point for the X path: later runs measure against these rows.

The two runs see the same difference images. SExtractor's counts agree
image by image (ratio median 1.000 on both signs), the loader rule keeps
the same rows to within a few per cent per image, and both catalogs find
the same injected sources (813 positive and 108 negative against Russ's
814 and 107). The alerts are made from different catalogs. The rebuild's
packets come from `xsources`, while Russ's reqid 8 alerts come from his
PSF-fit Sources, so the two alert streams are not a like-for-like
comparison. The gate held all 72 of the rebuild's packet sets, and none
reached the alert topic.

## Outcome against the truth

A truth row is detected when a catalog row of its exposure and SCA lies
within 0.3 arcsec, the nearest, each sign matched on its own. A false
positive is a catalog row with no truth row of its epoch within 0.3
arcsec. The rebuild's packets are scored at their trigger xsource's
position, the held ones included. Its `xsources` and `sources` are the
official rows after the prefilter.

On Russ's side the alerts are the diaSources of his reqid 8 Avro files.
His XSources are his `sfftdiffimage_masked{,_negative}.txt` catalogs
with `dev`'s loader rule applied by
`rapidpipe.science.difference.xsources` at run 169's thresholds, because
his loaded rows are in IMSS's database, which was not reachable for this
comparison. His Sources are his `sfftdiffimage_masked_psfcat{,_negative}.parquet`
catalogs under the rebuild's `sources` prefilter at run 169's values;
that prefilter adds a 2 px edge cut that `dev`'s PSF-fit path lacks.

Per image, the rebuild's packets detect 0 to 3 truth rows with 1,997 to
2,000 false positives, and Russ's alerts 0 to 8 with 1,971 to 2,000.
`completeness.py`'s own output for run 169 agrees with the rebuild's

0.3 arcsec of an unrelated detection 4% of the time, so from about AB 24
down every count is at that chance floor
([the window's completeness](2026-10-27-socsims-1021.md)).

The packets detect 49 truth rows where Russ's alerts detect 212 because
the two pick their 2,000 per chip from different catalogs by different
rules. On four images every one of Russ's alerts sits within 0.01 px of
a row of his PSF-fit catalog and on no SExtractor row, and its `snr`
equals the PSF fit's. His 2,000 are not the highest S/N of that catalog
either: the alerted rows' median S/N rank lies between 0.08 and 0.23 of
the catalog, and the alerts' S/N quantiles are 0, 3.85, 11.7, 293 and
3,694. The rule that chooses them was not read. The rebuild's 2,000 are
the highest absolute S/N of the image's unflagged xmembers (S/N 25 to
12,401, median 125), which in this field are the brightest residuals of
stars. On the catalogs both runs share, the detections are the same.

**What the ratios show.** The SExtractor read counts are 1.000 at the
median and differ on 45 positive images by up to 1.8%; that cause is
unread. Russ's positive sum equals his 10/20 pilot's published 4,988,914
exactly, while the rebuild reads 3,764 more than run 158's pilot did
(4,990,768), so the rebuild's difference images have changed since run
158. The references that differ from Russ's by `dev`'s `mjdobs`
tie-break (27 of 72 on run 158's pilot) are a candidate cause, not
tested.

The kept counts were read on eight images whose read counts are equal.
There the rows Russ keeps and the rebuild drops, 8 to 181 per image, pass
every cut on his catalog's own values. The rebuild recomputes the extra
columns, and its own values decide them: its rb differs from his by
more than 0.05 on 3 to 5% of the rows both keep, with a median
difference of 0.0006. The 2 to 8 rows per image that only the rebuild
keeps fail the 2 px edge cut at his positions. So the kept ratio's
departure from 1.000 comes from the recomputed rb and sumrat and the
rounding of the edge.

The aperture fluxes agree exactly, so the photometry differs only in the
PSF fit, and the wide spread on the negative side is the fit's. The PSF
is a known input difference (below); that it causes the spread is not
tested, as for run 158's 1.154. For reference, the rebuild keeps 537,292
positive and 248,063 negative `sources` against Russ's 488,922 and
203,584, 10% and 22% more, in the direction of run 158's PSF-fit
excess; that is unread too.

## The packets against the rebuild's own `xsources`

Run 169 has 2,103,835 xsources. Each of its 1,935,591 xmemberships
founds its own xobject, and every xmember has SExtractor flags 0. The
168,244 xsources that are not members all have nonzero flags, so the
unflagged xmembers are exactly the unflagged xsources. Each xobject has
one member because the slice's four visits of an SCA do not overlap:
on SCAs 1, 8 and 17 no later xsource lies within even 0.3 arcsec of an
earlier one.

All 144,000 packets sit on unflagged xmembers, with no duplicate trigger
and no packet without its xsource. In every one of the 72 sets the
packets are exactly the 2,000 highest absolute S/N (the fit's, else the
3 px aperture's) of that image's 7,911 to 67,198 unflagged xmembers
(median 24,273). The other 1,791,591 unflagged xmembers fall below the
cap and yield none, as `max_alerts` rules. 768 packets are negative, and
124 carry the aperture fallback for `psfFlux` because their source has
no usable fit.

The diaSource mapping validates on every packet. Rapid's
`scripts/read_packets.py` at `35766464` read all 72 sets clean: 144,000
index rows and 144,000 records, every record valid against its schema
version, 432,000 cutouts (23,811 at an edge), 143,251 forced-photometry
rows, no `prvDiaSources` because no earlier epoch overlaps, and no
failures, over 27.6 GB of Avro in 2,135 s. `scripts/validate_run.py --run 169`
at the same commit passed lineage (no orphans; 12,667 input rows and
3,070 bindings resolve) and tables (2,839 published jobs, 8,017,485
rows, counts equal, checksums match). It was stopped at 90 minutes,
before the catalog, forced, counts and packets checks; those four stand
on run 169's own `check_run.sh` on the fleet on 2026-11-08, validate 6
of 6 and reader 72 of 72 clean.

## The gate

The gate held all 72 sets: 71 on the core fraction (median +0.064,
+0.024 to +0.148 against the bound of ±0.025) and one on the ring. No
packet reached the alert topic, so the consumer had nothing to read and
`check_run.sh` reads FAIL on that check alone. Russ's run has no gate.

GBTDS gets bounds of its own stays on

## Known differences between the two systems

- **The alert catalog.** The rebuild's packets come from `xsources`, `xobjects` and `xmemberships`; Russ's reqid 8 alerts come from his PSF-fit Sources, as the ruling notes `dev`'s alerts do. Packets and alerts therefore describe different detections even on the same image.
- **Which 2,000.** The rebuild takes the highest absolute S/N; `dev`'s choice among its Sources is not the highest S/N and was not read.
- **The gate.** `dev` has none, so his alerts are all out; the rebuild holds every set of this slice.
- **The PSF.** The rebuild reduces the CRDS ePSF itself where `dev` reads Jacob's per-SCA PSF built from the same ePSFs. This changes every PSF fit and is the candidate for the `psfFlux` spread and the `sources` excess.
- **Recomputed extra columns.** The rebuild computes rb, sumrat and the pixel columns itself; rb differs from Russ's by more than 0.05 on 3 to 5% of the shared rows, which moves the prefilter's choice on a few per cent of rows.
- **The edge cut on `sources`.** The rebuild's `sources` prefilter applies the 2 px edge cut that `dev` applies to XSources only.
- **Reference tie-break.** `dev` breaks a tie in `mjdobs` by distance from the tile centre and the rebuild's loop does not. On run 158's pilot, the same slice, 27 of the 72 references differed from Russ's by one to three of 25 frames ([run 158's post](2026-10-27-socsims-1021.md)); reqid 8's references were not read.
- **Association.** The rebuild founds xobjects per difference by the source's field ({ref}`ruling <decision-xobjects>`); `dev` cross-matches XSources into XAstroObjects with a second pass. On this slice no xobject has a second member, and Russ's XAstroObjects were not read.

## What is not claimed

- **Which system alerts better.** The packets and the alerts are drawn from different catalogs by different rules, so 49 against 212 ranks neither.
- **XAstroObjects.** The xobjects are not compared with `dev`'s; the row is empty.
- **Russ's loaded rows.** His XSources here are his catalogs under the loader rule, not the rows his loader wrote.
- **The causes left unread:** the SExtractor read differences, the `psfFlux` spread, the `sources` excess and Russ's alert selection rule.
- **The `xsources` S/N value.** The prefilter stays at `dev`'s 5.0 until a run measures what it costs; this run does not measure that.
- **Faint completeness.** Every bin from about AB 24 down is at the chance floor at 0.3 arcsec in this field.
- **Breadth.** One slice of 72 images and four exposures, one run on each side.
