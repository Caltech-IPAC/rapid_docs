# Russ Laher's 10/21/2026 socsims run, reproduced on SMDC

On 2026-10-21 Russ Laher ran `dev`'s science pipeline over a 4.5 hour
window of the SOC GBTDS socsims simulation: 1,998 W146 images, 111
exposures of 18 SCAs, with the 109 references of his 10/20 test. On
2026-10-27 the rebuild processed the same 1,998 files on SMDC as run 158
(dataset `r00340-fakesrc-1021`), as close to his configuration as it
can get, so the two runs can be read side by side. This post is written
to be read next to his write-up of both tests. It gives his numbers
beside SMDC's wherever a row corresponds and writes "none" where one
pipeline has no counterpart.

Every image of the window has a published SFFT difference and a packet
set, and no job is left failed. The SFFT positive SExtractor counts
match Russ's to within 0.1% over the 1,998 images. The PSF-fit counts
are about 15% higher per image. The gate holds 1,997 of the 1,998 packet
sets on the core residual, so only one set reached the alert topic. The
images look like differences of the same scene on 13 of 15 inspected
panels; the other two are one SCA 2 pointing that Russ's own difference
shows the same way.

Six SCA 18 differences exited 70 on every attempt on `:96`. Their
pixel columns' 64 px local background failed: photutils 3.0 drops every
mesh box that keeps under 90% of its pixels after the 3-sigma clip, and
these heavy-tailed SFFT differences keep 88.5%. `dev` catches the same
failure and measures the columns on the image itself, which is why
Russ's 1,998 jobs all exited 0. Rapid PR 500 takes `dev`'s behaviour
({ref}`the pixel-columns ruling <decision-pixel-columns-background>`),
and all six published on `:97`. One packet job hit Batch's "Task failed
to start" and published on its retry. Separately, the run's reference
bound, 61685.81344, rounds the window's start up, so the loop treated the
window's first exposure (MJD 61685.8134375) as reference material and
never differenced it. Its 18 differences and 18 packet jobs were
submitted by hand under the run, and no reference took one of its
frames.

The gate holds 1,997 of the 1,998 sets: 1,993 on `core_fraction` and 4
on `ring_fraction`. The core is the fraction of a bright star's flux left
in its core after subtraction. Its median over the run is +0.066, and
1,993 of 1,998 images are outside the ±0.025 bound, which was derived on
HLWAS and stands for this run. The held sets stay in the official
views, built and unsent. The one set sent, packet job 31648
(r0034001005001003023_0001_wfi03_f146), had a difference with no residual
stars, so its core and ring were not computed, and the gate does not hold
on a metric the difference could not compute. On the pilot the gate held
all 72 sets (71 on the core, median +0.065; 1 on the ring).

## What differs from `dev`, and why

- **No fake-source recovery count.** The injection catalogs of the
  2026-08-07 fake-source set are not on SMDC, so no completeness is
  measured here; Russ's write-up has none for these two tests either.
- **The 500 cap.** The difference job keeps the 500 highest-S/N
  candidates after the prefilter, as on every dataset the rebuild runs;
  `dev` caps at 2,000 per chip at alert time. Packet counts are therefore
  about a fifth of Russ's alert counts.
- **The gate.** `dev` has none. The rebuild holds a packet set whose core,
  ring, registration or usable fraction is out of bounds; on this data
  under the socsims block that is almost every set, so the stream is held
  and the products are kept.
- **The missing DQ plane** is shared: both pipelines read the same lite
  files and both run `dev`'s extreme-artifact repair. Whether Russ's
  references carry the same dark pits is missing (not checked).
- **The PSF.** The rebuild reduces the CRDS ePSF itself where `dev` reads
  Jacob's per-SCA PSF built from the same ePSFs. On SCA 7 both peak at
  the same pixel, their centroids are 0.17 px apart, the summed absolute
  difference of the normalised stamps is 0.21, and the stage's stamp
  peaks 1.36 times higher, so it is the sharper of the two. The PSF-fit
  excess above may come from this; it is not tested.
- **Reference tie-break.** 27 of the pilot's 72 references differ from
  Russ's by one to three of 25 frames, from the tie-break in `mjdobs`
  above.
- **Post-processing.** The rebuild has no cross-match into AstroObjects,
  no statistics or pruning stage; its catalog association happens per
  difference ({ref}`association in the difference <decision-association-in-difference>`),
  548,449 objects in 358 fields for this run.
